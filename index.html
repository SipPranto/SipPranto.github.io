About

A civil engineer who found his way into data.

I studied civil engineering at BUET with a focus on transportation. Along the way I realised that the hardest questions about a city, like where people travel, where floods reach and how clean the air is, are really questions about data.

Today I apply geospatial analysis and machine learning to EU-funded projects, and my research interests include travel behavior, demand modelling, emissions and intelligent transportation systems.
